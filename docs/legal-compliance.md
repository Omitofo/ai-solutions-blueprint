# Legal, Security & Compliance

This is part of the product, not an afterthought — especially for Spanish and European SMEs.

---

## GDPR & Data Protection

Key questions for every client and workflow:

- Is personal data sent to external LLM providers?
- Is the provider acting as processor or controller?
- Do we have (and can we offer) a Data Processing Agreement?
- Where is data stored and processed (residency / transfers)?
- Retention and deletion policies
- Access permissions and auditability
- Special categories of data (health, financial, etc.)

**Practical stance:**  
For early products we prefer workflows that minimize unnecessary personal data in prompts, use redaction where possible, and keep clear records of what is processed where.

---

## EU AI Act (context as of late 2026)

Most provisions are applicable. Transparency and AI literacy obligations are relevant. High-risk system obligations have later dates for some cases.

**Our positioning for Phase 1:**
- AI as **assistant** for document preparation, classification, drafting, information retrieval, reconciliation, reporting
- Human remains responsible for final decisions on payments, tax treatment, legal advice, high-value approvals, etc.
- Clear documentation of what the system can and cannot do autonomously

We avoid selling fully autonomous high-risk decision systems until we have proper controls, documentation and professional oversight frameworks.

---

## Operational controls we require for every workflow

- Who is responsible for the decision?
- What can the AI execute independently?
- What requires explicit human approval?
- What happens when the AI is uncertain or confidence is low?
- How can an action be reversed or corrected?
- What evidence / logs are retained?

---

## Security basics

- Per-client credential isolation
- Least-privilege access to business systems
- Logging of actions and data access
- Ability to revoke access quickly
- Regular review of connected integrations

This document will grow into concrete policies, checklists and contract clauses as we move toward real clients.
