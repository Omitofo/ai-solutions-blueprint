# Industry Opportunity Map

We evaluate industries on:
- Volume of repetitive administrative work
- Volume of emails / documents / quotations
- Cost of employee time
- Existing software maturity
- Willingness to pay
- Complexity and risk of automation

---

## Priority candidates (Phase 1-2)

| Industry                  | Promising automations                          | Main value type     |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| Real estate agencies      | Lead qualification, enquiries, scheduling      | Sales + admin       |
| Construction              | Quotations, invoices, project reporting        | Administration      |
| Accountancy firms         | Document classification, client requests       | Productivity        |
| Legal / professional firms| Document search, summaries, admin              | Productivity        |
| Wholesale / distribution  | Orders, quotations, follow-ups                 | Sales + operations  |
| Small e-commerce          | Customer support, orders, product content      | Sales + service     |
| Tourism / hospitality     | Reservations, enquiries, guest communication   | Sales + service     |
| Clinics (dental, etc.)    | Appointments, reminders, admin                 | Time savings        |

---

## Secondary / later

- Restaurants (reservations + supplier invoices)
- Logistics (tracking + documentation)
- Manufacturing (reporting + quality docs)
- Consulting (research + proposals)

---

## Ideal early client characteristics

- Clear repetitive processes
- Digital tools already in use (Gmail/Outlook, some CRM or accounting software)
- Owner or manager who feels the pain of admin load
- Data that is mostly digital (not pure paper)
- Willingness to start with a limited scope pilot
- Reasonable labour cost (so hours saved have economic meaning)

We actively avoid early clients with pure legacy systems, very low process volume, or high regulatory risk without proper professional oversight.
