# Automation Catalogue (by business function)

We start from **functions**, not industries. Almost every business has a combination of these.

---

## 1. Administration & Communication
- Read and classify incoming emails
- Draft responses
- Organize attachments
- Schedule meetings / follow-ups
- Summarize conversations or threads
- Prepare daily activity reports

**Value type:** Time savings + reduced chaos  
**Broad applicability**

---

## 2. Sales & Commercial Operations
- Find / enrich potential customers
- Qualify leads
- Prepare quotations from templates + price lists
- Follow up on quotations
- Update CRM records
- Identify cross-sell / up-sell opportunities
- Generate simple sales forecasts

**Value type:** Revenue potential + capacity  
**High commercial attractiveness**

---

## 3. Finance & Accounting Administration
- Read invoices and receipts
- Extract supplier, tax, amount, date, categories
- Match invoices with payments
- Detect duplicates
- Prepare financial summaries
- Organize documentation for accountants

**Value type:** High automation potential + error reduction  
**Very measurable**

---

## 4. Customer Service
- Answer FAQs
- Track orders / appointments
- Handle simple requests
- Route complex cases
- Prepare personalized responses
- Escalate with context

**Value type:** Response time + service quality

---

## 5. Operations & Management
- Generate operational reports
- Track pending tasks and deadlines
- Coordinate simple workflows
- Identify bottlenecks
- Produce management dashboards / summaries

**Value type:** Visibility + decision support

---

## 6. Research & Compliance Support
- Search regulations or internal policies
- Compare supplier offers
- Summarize regulatory changes
- Prepare documentation packages
- Flag missing information

**Value type:** Productivity  
**Requires stronger human verification and controls**

---

## Prioritization shortlist (Phase 1)

1. Email & administrative assistant
2. Invoice / receipt processing
3. Quotation generation
4. CRM + lead follow-up
5. Customer service assistant
6. Management reporting (depends heavily on data quality)
7. Regulatory research (later, with stronger controls)

Each solution will eventually have:
- Standard workflow description
- Cost estimate (implementation + monthly)
- Implementation checklist
- KPI dashboard definition
- Risk / approval requirements
