# Commercial Packages

We sell **packages based on business outcomes**, not individual agents.

Prices below are **hypotheses** to be validated with pilots. All exclude VAT and major custom development.

---

## 1. Entry Package — AI Office Assistant

**For:** Small businesses / professionals (1-10 people)

**What it includes:**
- Email classification and drafting
- Calendar management and scheduling
- Document classification and organization
- Meeting summaries
- Simple daily/weekly administrative reports
- Basic knowledge base (policies, templates)

**Typical monthly operating cost (internal):** €100–250  
**Suggested selling price:** €149–299 / month  
**Implementation fee:** €600–1,500

**Main value sold:** Hours saved + faster response + less chaos

## Pricing logic (how we decide the number)

We do **not** simply mark up API costs.

Formula direction:

```
Selling price = (Infrastructure + Maintenance allocation + Support allocation) × target margin factor
```

Then we check against client value:

- Expected hours saved × loaded labour cost
- Additional gross profit from better sales/response
- Risk reduction

We only sell when the client has a clear positive ROI (ideally payback < 6 months on implementation + first months of subscription).

## Additional commercial elements

- Setup / implementation fee (one-time)
- Monthly subscription (base package)
- Usage allowance + overage charges (when volume is high)
- Custom development / extra integrations (time & materials or fixed)
- Premium support / dedicated success manager (higher tiers)

This structure protects margin when a client has unusually high volume or complex systems.
