# Vision & Positioning

## One-sentence positioning

**AI Operations Partner for SMEs** — we turn repetitive business processes into reliable, measurable AI-powered operations at a predictable monthly cost.

## What we sell

We do **not** sell:
- Chatbots for the sake of having a chatbot
- Generic AI tools
- One-off consulting that disappears after delivery

We **do** sell:
- Measurable improvements in operations (hours saved, faster response, more revenue, fewer errors)
- Reusable AI systems + agents + integrations
- Ongoing management of the AI infrastructure

## Target customers

Primary focus (Phase 1-2):
- Small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Spain / Europe
- Especially: real estate, construction, professional services (accountancy, legal, consulting), wholesale/distribution, small e-commerce, tourism, clinics

Secondary:
- Independent professionals (freelancers, small practices)
- Later: larger SMEs with multiple departments

## Core value proposition

> We analyse your processes, identify where AI generates clear economic value, implement the agents and integrations, and manage everything so you get results without becoming an AI company yourself.

## Differentiation

1. Outcome-based packaging (not agent-based)
2. Transparent cost model + ROI calculation before selling
3. Model-independent architecture (client is not locked to one LLM)
4. Strong focus on integration difficulty and data readiness (we say "no" when the case is weak)
5. Human-in-the-loop by design for high-risk decisions

## Long-term vision (3-5 years)

Become the default "AI operations layer" for mid-market companies in selected verticals in Spain and then expand in Europe, with a high percentage of standardized, productized solutions and a smaller high-margin custom practice.
