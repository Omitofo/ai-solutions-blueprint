# Business Case Template (per client / opportunity)

## Client overview
- Company, size, industry
- Current tools
- Main processes in scope

## Proposed solution
- Package or custom combination
- Agents / workflows included
- Integrations required (with difficulty level)

## Costs
### Implementation (one-time)
- Discovery & design
- Development & integrations
- Testing & deployment
- Training
- **Total implementation**

### Monthly operating (our cost)
- LLM / API
- Automation platform
- Hosting & data
- Monitoring
- Maintenance allocation
- Support allocation
- **Total monthly cost to us**

### Proposed commercial terms
- Setup fee
- Monthly subscription
- Usage / overage rules
- **Expected gross contribution**

## Client value
- Estimated hours saved / month
- Loaded labour cost
- Capacity or revenue impact
- Error / risk reduction (qualitative or quantitative)
- **Monthly economic benefit**
- **Net monthly benefit**
- **Payback period**
- **ROI (12 months)**

## Risks & dependencies
- Data readiness
- Integration difficulty
- Required human approvals
- Adoption risks
- Mitigation plan

## Recommendation
- Proceed / Proceed with conditions / Do not proceed
- Suggested pilot scope and success metrics
