# 05 — Who this is for

## Intent
Create recognition.  
“They work with businesses like mine.”

## Marketing role
Selective positioning. We are not for everyone, and that builds trust.

## Layout
Clean grid or horizontal row of industry cards (Real Estate, Construction, Accountancy, Legal, Wholesale, E-commerce, Tourism, Clinics…).

Each card:
- Industry name
- 1 short line of typical value (Sales / Administration / Productivity…)

## Tone
Quiet confidence. No “we serve everyone”.
