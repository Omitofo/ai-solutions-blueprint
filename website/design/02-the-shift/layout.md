# 02 — The Shift

## Intent
Make the visitor feel the difference between the current state and the possible state.

“There is a clear before and after.”

## Marketing role
Show the carrot by contrast. Minimal text. Strong visual comparison.

## Layout options
- 50/50 split (Before | After)
- Or a single strong visual with two short columns underneath

## Short copy direction

**Before**  
Manual follow-ups.  
Buried emails.  
Reports made by hand.  
Time spent on repetition.

**After**  
Agents that prepare.  
Systems that connect.  
Clear visibility.  
Time returned to your team.

## Visual direction
Clean, almost diagrammatic or scene-based.  
Avoid cartoonish. Keep it premium and calm.
