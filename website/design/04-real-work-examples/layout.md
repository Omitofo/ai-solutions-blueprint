# 04 — How it looks in real work

## Intent
Make the abstract concrete.  
Show specific situations the visitor recognizes from their own business.

## Marketing role
This is where imagination becomes tangible.  
“I can see this happening in my company.”

## Layout ideas
- Horizontal scroll or clean grid of 4–6 short scenes
- Or stacked 50/50 blocks (visual left / short text right)

## Example scenes (we will visualise these one by one later)
1. Email & communication assistant preparing replies and organising the inbox
2. Quotation being prepared from templates + price list
3. Invoices being read and organised automatically
4. Daily/weekly operational summary ready for the manager
5. Lead follow-up happening without manual chasing

## Copy style
Very short labels + one line of outcome.  
Almost no paragraphs.
