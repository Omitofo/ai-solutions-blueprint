# Website Design Plan — BRAIN AI Solutions

## Marketing philosophy

Three levers:

1. **Urgency with inspiration** (FOMO rítmico)  
2. **Clarity through contrast** (Before → After)  
3. **Authority through education** (tokens, costes, escenarios)

### Emotional rhythm

- **Hero already contains the first FOMO** (highest or second hierarchy)
- Then we lift with hope and vision
- Every 2–3 sections we place a short FOMO quote as reminder
- Never overwhelm — just keep the tension alive while delivering the promise

**Wave:**  
FOMO (in Hero) → Up → Mild FOMO reminder → Up → Mild FOMO reminder → Up → Close with direction
