# 03 — What becomes possible

## Intent
Show the outcomes the client actually cares about.  
Not features. Not “AI agents”. Results.

## Marketing role
This is pure carrot. Help them picture the improved state of their business.

## Layout
Short horizontal row of 3 (max 4) cards or visual scenes.

Possible pillars:
1. Drive Efficiency → less repetitive work
2. Unlock Insights → clearer decisions
3. Accelerate Growth → more capacity and faster response

## Short copy style
Title + one short sentence maximum per card.

Example:
**More capacity**  
Your team spends time on work that moves the business, not on chasing information.

## Visual direction
Light cards, soft icons or small scenes.  
Very clean. No long paragraphs.
