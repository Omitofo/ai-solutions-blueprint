# 07 — Choose your starting point (Packages)

## Intent
Present the three packages as practical starting points, not as a hard sell.

Visitor feeling:  
“There is a clear path that matches my size and needs.”

## Presentation style
We show them through **use cases / scenarios**, not feature dumps.

### Package 1 — Office Assistant
**For:** Small teams and independent professionals  
**Typical start:** Email + calendar + light document organisation + daily summary  
**Value:** Hours returned + less chaos

### Package 2 — Business Operations
**For:** Growing SMEs  
**Typical start:** Office Assistant + quotations + invoice organisation + CRM follow-up + weekly reports  
**Value:** Capacity + commercial support

### Package 3 — Business Infrastructure
**For:** More complex or multi-department needs  
**Typical start:** Multiple specialised agents + deeper integrations + governance  
**Value:** Operational leverage across the company

## Layout
Three clean cards, outcome-first language, soft CTAs.

## Visual assets
- 3 package cards
- Optional small scenario illustration per card
