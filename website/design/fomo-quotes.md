# FOMO Quotes Bank

Short interstitial statements used across the homepage to keep a rhythmic sense of urgency without overwhelming the visitor.

## Opening FOMO (Section 02 — Ride the wave)

**Headline**  
Don’t get left behind.

**Supporting**  
Integrate intelligent systems and ride the wave of the future.

## Interstitial quotes (place every 2–3 sections)

These appear as clean, full-width or centered statement blocks with lots of whitespace.

1. **The gap is already opening.**  
   The only question is which side you build on.

2. **While some keep doing everything manually,**  
   others are already designing the systems that will carry them.

3. **Staying still is also a decision.**

4. **The future is not waiting.**  
   It is already being integrated.

5. **Every month without better systems**  
   is another month of the same weight.

6. **Adaptation is not optional.**  
   It is just a matter of when — and how deliberately.

---

## Placement suggestion

- After Section 04 (What becomes possible) → Quote 1 or 2
- After Section 06 (Understand the costs) → Quote 3 or 5
- Optional soft line before Final invitation → Quote 4 or 6

## Visual treatment

- Very clean
- Large typography
- Minimal or no illustration
- Same white AI Lab background
- Feels like an editorial statement, not an ad
