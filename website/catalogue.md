# Automation Catalogue

We organise solutions by **business function**. Almost every company has a combination of these.

Each item below can become one or more specialised agents that work with your existing tools.

---

## 1. Administration & Communication

**Typical agents / capabilities**
- Classify and prioritise incoming emails
- Draft replies in your tone
- Organise attachments and documents
- Schedule meetings and send confirmations
- Follow up on pending items
- Summarise long threads or meetings
- Prepare daily or weekly activity reports

**Main value:** Time savings + reduced administrative load  
**Best for:** Almost any business

---

## 2. Sales & Commercial Operations

**Typical agents / capabilities**
- Qualify and enrich leads
- Prepare quotations from templates and price lists
- Follow up on open quotations
- Update CRM records automatically
- Detect cross-sell / up-sell opportunities
- Generate simple pipeline or forecast summaries

**Main value:** Revenue support + faster commercial cycles  
**Best for:** Companies with regular quotations and lead volume

---

## 3. Finance & Accounting Administration

**Typical agents / capabilities**
- Read invoices and receipts
- Extract supplier, amounts, dates, tax and categories
- Match invoices with payments
- Flag duplicates or anomalies
- Organise documentation for your accountant
- Prepare period summaries

**Main value:** High automation potential + fewer errors  
**Best for:** Any business with regular supplier invoices

---

## 4. Customer Service

**Typical agents / capabilities**
- Answer frequent questions
- Track order or appointment status
- Handle simple requests
- Route complex cases with full context
- Prepare personalised responses
- Escalate when confidence is low

**Main value:** Faster response times + consistent service quality  
**Best for:** Businesses with high volume of similar enquiries

---

## 5. Operations & Management

**Typical agents / capabilities**
- Generate operational reports
- Track pending tasks and deadlines
- Surface bottlenecks
- Produce management summaries and dashboards
- Coordinate simple internal workflows

**Main value:** Visibility and better decision support  
**Best for:** Companies that currently compile reports manually

---

## 6. Research & Compliance Support

**Typical agents / capabilities**
- Search internal policies or external regulations
- Compare supplier offers
- Summarise changes in rules or contracts
- Prepare documentation packages
- Flag missing information

**Main value:** Productivity for knowledge work  
**Note:** Always designed with stronger human verification

---

## How we use this catalogue

In discovery we map your real processes against this catalogue, estimate volume and complexity, and recommend the combination that produces the clearest economic return.
