# Industries We Serve

We focus on businesses where repetitive processes, document volume and customer interactions create clear opportunities for AI agents.

---

## Priority industries

| Industry                    | Example automations                                      | Main value              |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| **Real Estate**             | Lead qualification, property enquiries, appointment scheduling | Sales + Administration |
| **Construction**            | Quotations, invoices, project reporting                  | Administration          |
| **Accountancy firms**       | Document classification, client request handling         | Productivity            |
| **Legal / Professional services** | Document search, case summaries, admin workflows   | Productivity            |
| **Wholesale & Distribution**| Orders, quotations, customer follow-ups                  | Sales + Operations      |
| **E-commerce**              | Customer support, order status, product content support  | Sales + Service         |
| **Tourism & Hospitality**   | Reservations, guest enquiries, communication             | Sales + Service         |
| **Clinics (dental, etc.)**  | Appointments, reminders, administrative tasks            | Time savings            |

---

## What we look for in a good fit

- Recurring administrative or commercial processes
- Digital tools already in place (email, CRM, accounting software…)
- Enough volume for automation to matter
- Willingness to start with a focused pilot
- Data that is mostly digital (not pure paper)

We are selective. If the numbers or the systems do not support a good outcome, we say so.
