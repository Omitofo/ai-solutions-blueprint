Raw space for ideas, hypotheses and possible directions.

## Product ideas

- "AI Operations Scorecard" as a free/paid diagnostic tool before any implementation (helps qualification and creates pipeline)
- Pre-built connectors + playbooks specifically for Holded, Odoo, Sage, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace + WhatsApp Business (Spanish SME stack)
- Vertical packs (e.g. "Real Estate AI Ops Pack") that combine the standard solutions with industry-specific templates
- Usage-based "agent hours" or "task credits" on top of the base package for high-volume clients

## Go-to-market ideas

- Partner with accountancy firms or business consultants who already have SME relationships
- Offer a paid 2-week discovery + business case as the first product (low commitment, high value)
- Content around "real cost of running AI agents" and transparent ROI calculators (builds trust)

## Technical ideas

- Internal "agent factory" that generates n8n workflows + evaluation suites from high-level descriptions
- Shared evaluation dataset per automation type so we can measure quality over time
- Cost anomaly alerts (sudden increase in tokens or failures per client)

## Business model experiments

- Success-fee component on top of subscription for clear revenue-impact use cases (quotations, lead conversion)
- White-label option later for larger partners
